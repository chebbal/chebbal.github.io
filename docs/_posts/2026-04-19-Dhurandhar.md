Dhurandhar has hit a bulls eye in terms of writing adn execution, that boldly takes a stand and fleshes out the content based on it's belief. It beautifully links the rela-world events with suitable connectives(real and imagined) to make the significant events(IC814, Parliament attack, 26/11, demonetisation, Khalistan etc) more relatable and understandable to the normal public. This series of films will be on my rewatch list for yeasr to come. There were many standout moments, but the two moments that stood out for me are the 1. The conversation between Jaskirat and Sanyal at the beginning and 2. The conversation between Sanyal and ISI chief at the end, both from the second film of the series.

# Conversation between Jaskirat and Sanyal
Context: Jaskirat is abducted from police prison transfer and is brought to an undisclosed location. Sanyal is working on operation Dhurandhar and is interested to recruit Jaskirat for the same. 

Sanyal's sub-ordinate looks at jaskirat's file and is amazed at the courage of the guy to go after a sitting MLA and kill his entire bloodline in act of revenge to avenge the datasrdly killing of his father and molestation of his sisters, when the system that is supposed to protect them, sided with those in power. Sanyals subordinate makes a light remark on the act of revenge, to which jaskirat's responds stating that revenge is not everyone's cup of tea. **It needs certain kind of courage to act on the pain** and not everyone has it.

It's at this moment, sanyal enters into the scene. He offers an option to jaskirat to commit his life to the country and sanyal will take care of his conviction and also provide a means substinence to his mother via pension. Jaskirat rejects the offer stating that although he is from a family of patriots, that did not help him or his family in their time of need and he cannot stand up to work for a system that in first place did not offer safety to his mother. He ends with a line stating that the love for the country is dead, the olde patriot jaskirat is dead and he is only biding his time to come to meet his maker, this can be seen as a parallel to arjuna giving up his will to fight in first chapter of gita. 

It is at this moment, Sanyal quotes a verse from Guru Gobind Singh **"Sooraa So Pehchaaniye Je Lade Deen Ke Haet. Purjaa Purjaa Katt Maray Kabahoo Na Chhaadday Khet."** This verse translates to **"A true warrior, one who upholds dharma never wavers from his path even in face of death"**. Sanyal follows it up with followig lines **"We are men Jaskirat, it is our duty to fight for our values, our dreams and our family and we should never expect anything in return. This what we are born to do."** Sanyal echoing the timeless teaching of krishna's call to action without any expectations on the outcome.

# Conversation between Sanyal and ISI chief

While the first conversation was personal and spiritual, this second conversation beautifully captures the change in power dynamics in the conversation flow

Context: Jaskirat is arrested and is being tortured by ISI to get him to confess that he is an Indian agent and list out the acts he has committed.

ISI chief recieves call from Sanyal, when he picks up the phone, he makes sure that sanyal can hear jaskirat being tortured. He picks up teh call and addresses him as Sanyal and tells him its been a while since we talked. Sanyal begins with greeting "Asalam walequm" and asks him to handover Jaskirat right now, when the word has not yet spread of his capture. ISI chief declines and states he now has got a golden duck and he will make it quack soon and use him to defame India on a global stage. Sanyal, calmly tells him that Jaskirat is not the one to breakdown so easily. ISI chief rejects the request stating that he is doing a jihad and he will achieve. Sanyal non-chalantly informs him that is he is wasting his time and most importantly sanyal's time with all the talk.

Sanyal now draws his trump card, he tells a story of a senior ISI operative that leaked out nuclear program secrets to Israelis. He then sends the video footage of the said deal. Turns out, the operative is none other than ISI chief himself.

From this point onwards, ISI chief addresses Sanyal as Sanyal Saab. The balance of power now just got inverted. He now reasons to sanyal that he cannot release Jaskirat as word has already spread of capture of an Indian spy in teh defense circles. If he has to release him, he has to pin the blame on someone. At this point, Jaskirat speaks up and tells him that he can blame this on Uzair who he has kept alive for this reason alone. Sanyal hears this and tell the chief that he has no choice but to do as he says and cuts the call.

This sequence perfectly captures the changing power dynamics in a matter of a single phone call. There are more such moments in this film and that shows the depth of writing and research that has gone into the making of this film. A true masterclass in weaving multiple layers into story-telling.
