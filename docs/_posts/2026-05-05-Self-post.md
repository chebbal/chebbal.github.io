This is the post I will point back to.

From this moment, **my only focus is my mission.**

I am done executing other people's half-baked visions. Done being a cog in wheels I didn't build, toward destinations I didn't choose. The work was real, the effort was real — but the ceiling was always someone else's.

That ends here.

I will build the wheel. I will build the chariot. And I will ride it.

This is not a pivot. It is not a plan. It is a declaration — a stake driven into the ground so that everything that comes after has something to measure itself against.

**Milestone Zero. The rest begins now.**
