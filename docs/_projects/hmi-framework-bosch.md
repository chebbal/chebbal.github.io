During my tenure at **Robert Bosch**, I focused on the development of high-performance **HMI widgets** and the underlying framework support for automotive infotainment systems.

### Technical Focus:
*   **Widget Development:** Built reusable, high-performance UI components for dashboard and center-console displays.
*   **Framework Architecture:** Contributed to the associated framework to ensure smooth interaction and efficient rendering of graphical elements in real-time automotive environments.
*   **Platform Support:** Ensured cross-platform compatibility and stability for large-scale automotive deployments.
