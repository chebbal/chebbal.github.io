Leveraging my industry experience, I designed a specialized **AD/ADAS curriculum** for a leading private university's B.Tech program. This initiative was aimed at closing the gap between academic theory and industry requirements in autonomous driving.

### Key Deliverables:
*   **Curriculum Development:** Built the course structure from the ground up, covering fundamental ADAS sensors, algorithms, and simulation methodologies.
*   **Staff Training:** Trained university teaching staff to ensure sustainable delivery of the technical content.
*   **Hands-on Learning:** Integrated industry-standard simulation tools into the coursework to provide students with practical experience.
