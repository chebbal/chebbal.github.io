My Master's thesis research at **Bonn-Rhein-Sieg University**, conducted in collaboration with **Elektrobit**, focused on the intersection of deep learning and sensor modeling.

### Technical Depth & Execution:
*   **Research Focus:** Investigated the feasibility of learning **inverse sensor models** directly from raw, noisy sensor data.
*   **Benchmarking:** Evaluated the performance of learned deep-learning models relative to traditional, hand-crafted analytical physical models.
*   **Engineering Impact:** Provided insights into data-driven sensor modeling for next-generation perception pipelines.
*   **Stack:** Python, Deep Learning (TensorFlow/Pytorch), Sensor Data Analytics.
