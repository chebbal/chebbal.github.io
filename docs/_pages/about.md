I work at the intersection of robotics, AI systems, edge computing, and hardware-aware software.

My career has taken me from building robotic systems and autonomous platforms to perception, real-time systems, edge inference, and increasingly toward the compilers and infrastructure underneath AI.

This site is my engineering notebook. I write here to think out loud.

I write about systems I build, technologies I study, papers I read, and ideas that influence how I approach engineering.

I am particularly interested in one question:

**How do we build machines that can perceive, reason, and act reliably under real-world constraints?**

Outside direct technical work, my influences are deeply civilizational and historical: the Ramayana, Indian history, philosophy, leaders, books, and first-principles engineering practice. I try to use these as lenses for judgment, not as motivational slogans.
